Basically this is just a small project of mine meant to help me learn more about Unity and to generally become a better Dev.
Unlike some other of my projects, this one I will actually not use AI to make code for me, like at all. 
The only times I will be using AI will be when I search up something I don't yet know or if I'm encountering a problem that I genuinely can't overcome alone.
Though I usually just search up Tutorials so... - the point is that there won't be any AI work in my project.

I asked a friend to give me a Game Jam type Topic, and he came up with "NOTHING". So here is the Game Idea I came up with:
        NOTHINGism - a game where a Mysterious Man assigns you tasks, always about wiping out something (places/people/communities...). 
        You are the NOTHINGer, and must follow the MMs assignments to earn money, not because you believe what you are doing is right, but because you need the money for your family. 
        By the end, you finally save up the amount of money you've been working hard for, but theres nothing to spend it on; no shops, no small family cafes, no services to pay for, NOTHING.

I believe I might be able to Complete the Game, which is the whole point of this - to finally complete an original game by myself.
